Drawing with Turtle

Python comes with a little robot turtle holding a pen. Tell it to move and turn, and it draws. With loops, functions and a few colors, you can make amazing art. This is the most visual lesson in the course.

  • Moving and turning

Meet the turtle

First you import turtle (you'll learn about import in the next lesson) and make a turtle. Then you give it commands:

CommandDoes
t.forward(n) / t.backward(n)Move n steps (pixels)
t.left(deg) / t.right(deg)Turn by some degrees (90 is a corner, 180 turns around, 360 is a full circle)
t.penup() / t.pendown()Lift the pen to move without drawing / put it back down
t.goto(x, y)Go straight to a point
t.circle(r)Draw a circle with radius r
t.dot(size, color)Stamp a filled dot
t.color("red") / t.width(5)Pen color / pen thickness
t.speed(0)0 is fastest. 1 is slowest, 10 is fast.
t.hideturtle()Hide the turtle so only the drawing shows

Where is the turtle? Coordinates

The drawing area is a grid. The middle is (0, 0). x goes left (−) and right (+). y goes down (−) and up (+). In this course the area is 400 wide and 300 tall, so x goes from −200 to 200 and y from −150 to 150.

Colors and filled shapes

To fill a shape with color: choose a fillcolor, call begin_fill(), draw the shape, then call end_fill(). You can use color names, or hex codes like "#FF5C8A" (the same as in the HTML course).

Common mistakes

  • A line where you didn't want one: use penup() before moving, and pendown() before drawing again.
  • Fill doesn't work: you need both begin_fill() before and end_fill() after the shape.
  • Drawing off the edge: keep x between −200 and 200, and y between −150 and 150.
  • Too slow: add t.speed(0) at the top.

To draw a triangle, how many degrees should the turtle turn at each corner?

The turtle must turn 360° in total over the 3 corners: 360 ÷ 3 = 120.

Remember

  • import turtle, then t = turtle.Turtle().
  • forward, left, right, goto(x, y), penup/pendown, circle, dot.
  • fillcolor + begin_fill() + shape + end_fill() for filled shapes.
  • Loops and small angle changes make beautiful patterns.
